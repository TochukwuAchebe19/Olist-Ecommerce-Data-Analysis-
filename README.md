## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset using **MySQL, Excel, and Tableau** to explore sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, and delivery efficiency.

- MySQL: Used JOINs, aggregations, and subqueries to analyze revenue, customer purchases, and product performance.
- Excel: Used PivotTables, XLOOKUP, and conditional formatting to organize data and perform additional analysis.
- Tableau: Built an interactive dashboard to visualize sales trends, customer segments, product performance, and delivery times.

# Key Findings

- Sales Performance: The dataset recorded approximately R$16.01 million in revenue, with an average order value of R$162.25.
- Customer Behavior: One-time customers accounted for approximately 94% of total revenue, while repeat customers contributed around 6%.
- Product Performance: Revenue varied across product categories, with some generating higher revenue despite lower order volumes.
- Regional Sales: Revenue and delivery times differed across Brazilian states, highlighting differences in purchasing activity and shipping efficiency.

# Real-World Business Impact
- These findings can help an e-commerce business make better decisions about customer retention, marketing, inventory, and logistics.

- Increasing Customer Retention: With approximately 94% of revenue coming from one-time customers, businesses could introduce loyalty programs and personalized promotions to encourage repeat purchases. For example, generating just 5% additional revenue from existing customers could potentially add **R$800,500** in sales, based on the R$16.01 million revenue figure.

- Improving Sales Performance: With an average order value of R$162.25, increasing that amount by 10% through product bundles, upselling, or targeted promotions would raise it to approximately **R$178.48**. If order volume remained unchanged, this could generate approximately **R$1.60 million in additional revenue**.

- Optimizing Product Inventory: By identifying which product categories generate the most revenue, businesses can prioritize high-performing products and reduce investment in slower-moving inventory. For example, reducing inventory-related costs by 5% on a hypothetical R$500,000 inventory budget would save **R$25,000**.

- Improving Delivery Efficiency: Analyzing delivery times across states helps businesses identify regions experiencing slower shipments. For example, reducing shipping costs by R$2 per order across approximately 98,666 orders could save around **R$197,332**.

# Project Takeaway
This project demonstrates how data analysis can go beyond identifying trends to support real business decisions. By combining SQL, Excel, and Tableau, I was able to analyze customer behavior, sales performance, and operational efficiency while identifying opportunities for revenue growth, cost reduction, and improved customer satisfaction.
